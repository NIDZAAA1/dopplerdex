# Responsible use

dopplerdex can estimate where a recording was made, sometimes to within tens of metres. Applied to
someone else's audio (a video, a voice message, a livestream) that can reveal where they live or where
they were. That is the harm this tool can do, and it is why this document exists.

## Do

- Analyse recordings **you made yourself**, or recordings whose owner explicitly agreed.
- Use public datasets whose publishers released the location along with the audio (field-recording
  archives, research soundscape datasets) and respect their licences.
- Use it to study the method, test ADS-B coverage, verify claims about your own recordings, or teach
  acoustics and signal processing.

## Do not

- Locate private individuals, streamers, content creators, or anyone who has not agreed to it.
- Use it to stalk, harass, dox, intimidate or surveil anyone.
- Publish locations derived from other people's recordings.

## If you publish recordings

The inverse is useful too: a recording with aircraft audible in it can leak your location. If that
matters to you, remove or mask sections with aircraft sound, or avoid publishing long outdoor audio
together with the date and time.

## Reporting misuse

If you learn that this software is being used to harm someone, please contact the maintainer
(see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private channels).
