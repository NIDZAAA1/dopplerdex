At any moment you hear sound that left the aircraft earlier, at the emission time t_e, solved from t_r = t_e + |O − S(t_e)| / c (with wind, the ray rides the moving air). The received frequency is f = f₀ · (c + n·w) / (c + n·w − n·v) evaluated at t_e. The rose curve uses the aircraft's state at the reception time instead: it is a different curve, not a delayed copy, which is why a naive model fails. With the ground reflection on, the broadband engine noise shows interference fringes whose spacing depends on mic height and elevation angle (Lloyd's mirror).
